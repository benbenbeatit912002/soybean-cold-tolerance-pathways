2. FOSCO correction and saved numerical confirmation

Import script lines153/154 select short score column2 and SNP column5.
Lines161-170 use lambda0.01..1.00 and
  corrected = 1 - (1 - raw_p)^(SNP^lambda)
while minimizing absolute Spearman correlation(corrected,SNP).
Lines172-174 write 數據/外部基因校正/Yamasaki_Go校正後1213.txt.
The saved snippet explicitly assigns only the short column. Nevertheless,
saved artifacts prove that BOTH phases were corrected: across all42,574
positive-SNP rows, the short column matches lambda0.01 and the middle column
matches lambda0.47, with maximum absolute differences6.11e-16 and5.56e-16.
Recomputing the grid on saved raw data selects exactly0.01 and0.47 under
the SNP-Spearman objective. This is numerical corroboration of the branch,
not a claim that the incomplete snippet alone executes the complete process.

Competing fragments are distinguishable:

  minimizes Spearman correlation with gene LENGTH, not SNP count.
- 數據/外部基因校正/圖片/go_yamasaki_intersection/討論後/新增 文字文件.txt
  lines8-15 uses Pearson correlation with SNP count; Spearman is commented.
  It assigns middle column3. Its input objects are not defined in that file.

3. Logarithmic artifacts and exceptions

程式/校正前後的繪圖.txt:4 and36 explicitly compute -log(p,base=10)
before/after correction for plotting. The older
篩選後/SUMSTAT/程式.txt:5-7,13-15 converts phase-specific outside scores
to -log10 and creates all_genes_short_outside_log / middle equivalents.
Its comment says outside_data is corrected, but its source is not loaded
within that fragment. Lines17-19 replace middle infinity with15.653559774527.

The principal saved Yamasaki_Go校正後1213_G_log10.txt contains42,575rows.
After canonical gene/occurrence matching,85,149 finite short/middle values
match -log10 of full-precision Yamasaki_Go校正後1213.txt to <5e-8.
It should not be regenerated by directly logging the rounded _G.txt variant,
which has both precision loss and different exceptional manual values.
The _loge.txt variant matches -ln of the full-precision corrected file to
<3.72e-7 for85,147 numeric cells; it contains three #NUM! cells.

Exceptions requiring an explicit reproduction policy:
- Glyma.14g129500, physical row29915 in raw/corrected/_G/_loge:
  raw SNP=0, scores0.804571275/0.512608881;
  corrected SNP=12, scores0.9947477/0.9008147;
  _G SNP=10, scores0.976596904/0.808522166;
  _loge SNP=0, both scores #NUM!;
  _log10 physical row11 uses SNP12 and0.002287056/0.045364535,
  agreeing with -log10 of the full corrected values.
- Glyma.04g222100, physical row7980 in raw/corrected/_G/_loge:
  raw middle p=5.21e-17; naive FOSCO subtraction produces stored0;
  _G middle value is1.07847e-16; _loge is #NUM!;
  _log10 physical row32950 uses15.47747. This finite replacement is not
  the direct logarithm of either stored0 or the _G middle value.
